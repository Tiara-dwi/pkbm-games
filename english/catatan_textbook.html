🧭 1. The 6 Master Prepositions of Place

Prepositions describe where an object or person is located relative to reference furniture in a room.

Preposition Meaning (Arti) Example Sentence Spatial Clue
IN Di dalam "The owl is in the toy chest." Surrounded inside an enclosure or container.
ON Di atas (menempel) "The safety helmet is on the study desk." Touching the top surface of furniture.
UNDER Di bawah "The skateboard is under the bed." Directly below another object or shelter.
BESIDE / NEXT TO Di sebelah / samping "Aqlan is standing beside the ramp." Side by side, with little space between.
BEHIND Di belakang "The water bottle is behind the tree." At the back side, partially hidden from front view.
IN FRONT OF Di depan "The knee pads are in front of the wardrobe." Facing forward, ahead of another object.

⚖️ 2. The Golden Grammar Formula: "There is" vs "There are"

Formula When to Use Examples in Daily Life
There is + a/an [Singular] Used when there is only ONE (1) object or item. • "There is an owl inside the box."
• "There is a skateboard under the wooden bed."
• "There is a study lamp on the desk."
There are + [Number / Plural -s/-es] Used when there are TWO OR MORE (2+) items. • "There are two pillows on the mattress."
• "There are five English books in the bookcase."
• "There are three owls on the tree branch."
⚠️ Crucial Trap to Avoid:
❌ "There is two skateboards under the bed." (Salah: subjek jamak tidak boleh menggunakan 'is')
✅ "There are two skateboards under the bed." (Benar: subjek jamak 2 skateboard memerlukan 'are')

💬 4. Daily Spatial Dialogue Patterns

Q1: Asking Object Location:
— Teacher: "Where is the safety helmet?"
— Aqlan: "It is on the study desk."

Q2: Asking Existence (Singular):
— Teacher: "Is there an owl in the toy chest?"
— Aqlan: "Yes, there is!" or "No, there isn't."

Q3: Asking Existence (Plural):
— Teacher: "Are there two pillows on the bed?"
— Aqlan: "Yes, there are!"

🎯 5. Master Challenge C3–C4 Self-Quiz

No Problem Statement Answer & Explanation
1 "Where is the skateboard? It is ... the bed." (Posisi persis di bawah dipan tempat tidur) under
'Under' menandakan benda berada di kolong / bawah objek lain.
2 "... (There is / There are) three storybooks on the table." There are
Tiga buku ('three storybooks') adalah plural/jamak, wajib memakai 'There are'.
3 "Where is the safety helmet? It is ... the study desk." (Posisi diletakkan di permukaan meja) on
'On' menandakan benda menempel pada permukaan atas meja.

💬 2. Doctor-Patient Clinical Dialogue Pattern

Step 1: Doctor asks the complaint
— Dr. Mishka: "What's the matter with you, Made?" (Ada apa denganmu?)

Step 2: Patient describes the condition
— Patient Made: "I have got a stomachache. My stomach hurts after eating spicy snacks."

Step 3: Doctor delivers medical verdict & advice
— Dr. Mishka: "You should take this medicine and drink plenty of warm water. You should not eat too much spicy food."

💊 3. Prescription Formula: "Should" vs "Should Not (Shouldn't)"

Modal Verb Function & Formula Clinical Prescription Example
SHOULD Subject + should + Verb 1
(Anjuran positif / hal yang wajib dilakukan demi kesembuhan)		 • "You should see a dentist."
• "He should take a rest in bed."
• "She should drink warm tea with honey."
SHOULD NOT
(SHOULDN'T)		 Subject + shouldn't + Verb 1
(Pantangan medis / larangan keras agar penyakit tidak memburuk)		 • "You should not eat too much candy."
• "He shouldn't drink icy beverages."
• "She shouldn't run on an injured foot."
⚠️ Grammar Rule: Modal verb should / shouldn't selalu diikuti Verb 1 murni (Bare Infinitive) tanpa tambahan -s, -ed, atau to!
❌ "He should to takes medicine."
✅ "He should take medicine."
