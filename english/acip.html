🏡 Case Study: The Detective Bedroom & First-Aid Kit

Acip and Kinanti are exploring the cozy detective bedroom. In the bedroom, there is a comfortable bed, a wooden wardrobe, and a study desk. Beside the bed, there is a small nightstand with an alarm clock. Under the bed, Acip spots two cool skateboards! On the study desk, there is an English textbook. Meanwhile, on the top shelf of the bookshelf, there is a red first-aid kit ready for any emergency. Hootie the pet owl is sitting quietly behind the open door!

Challenge 1: Preposition Radar! Where are the two skateboards located in the bedroom?
Challenge 2: Grammar Rule! Complete the sentence for ONE nightstand: "______ a small nightstand beside the bed."
Challenge 3: Plural Form! How do you say there are multiple skateboards under the bed?
Challenge 4: Hidden Pet! Where is Hootie the owl sitting according to the story?
Challenge 5: Multigrade Link! Where is the emergency first-aid kit placed?
