🍳 Case Study: Hasyif and Nanda's Morning in the Kitchen

Every Sunday morning, Hasyif and Nanda help their parents in the kitchen. In the clean kitchen, there is a big stove, a frying pan, and a white refrigerator beside the dining table. Nanda is washing two green apples in the sink, while Hasyif is preparing a glass of warm milk. On the table, there are plates, spoons, and a loaf of bread. Hasyif says, "Cooking together is fun, but we must be careful with the hot stove and sharp knives!"

Challenge 1: Which sentence correctly uses the Present Continuous Tense (is/are + Verb-ing) to describe an ongoing action?
Challenge 2: Preposition Analysis! Where is the white refrigerator located according to the text?
Challenge 3: Cause-and-Effect Logic! Why does Hasyif remind Nanda to be careful in the kitchen?
Challenge 4: Vocabulary Categorization! Which pair contains only cooking appliances and storage?
Challenge 5: Sentence Construction! If Hasyif is making warm milk right now, what is the correct sentence?
