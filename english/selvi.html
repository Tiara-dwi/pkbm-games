📜 Clinical Log: Medical Center Activity Last Week

Last week, Selvi reviewed the health clinic logs. On Monday, Shan was sick with a high fever and headache. He was in bed all morning. On Tuesday afternoon, gymnast Nia was injured during vault landing; her ankle was swollen and painful. On Wednesday, the 6th grade students were cold and exhausted after camping in the mountain. Fortunately, they were not careless. After following Dr. Ardiansyah's advice, Shan was relieved and the students were happy and energized again by Friday!
