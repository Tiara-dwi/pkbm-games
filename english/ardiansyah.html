🍜 Case: Aditya at the School Food Fair

During the school culinary festival, Aditya visits the canteen. At the food stall, Aditya orders a hot bowl of bakso and a fresh glass of iced tea. The soup tastes savory and salty, while the iced tea tastes sweet. Aditya also buys a bar of chocolate and a bottle of mineral water for his friend. Liquids and mass foods cannot be counted one by one, so we must use measuring containers!

Challenge 1: How do we correctly quantify hot bakso soup in English?
Challenge 2: How do we correctly order iced tea?
Challenge 3: Taste Vocabulary! How does Aditya's warm bakso soup taste?
Challenge 4: Which quantifier matches a piece of solid chocolate?
Challenge 5: Grammar Rule! Why do water, tea, and soup need quantifiers like 'a glass of' or 'a bowl of'?
