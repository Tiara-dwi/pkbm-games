🩺 Clinical Case: Dr. Ardiansyah's Busy Consultation Room

At the PKBM health clinic, Dr. Ardiansyah examines two patients. First is Jordan, who is holding his swollen cheek. Jordan has got a toothache because he ate too much sticky candy and forgot to brush his teeth. Dr. Ardiansyah writes on the prescription pad: 'Jordan should brush his teeth twice a day and he shouldn't eat candy.' The second patient is Nia, a gymnast who fell during practice. Nia has got a sprained ankle. Dr. Ardiansyah advises: 'She should compress her ankle with an ice pack and she shouldn't run for one week!' Remember the Golden Medical Law: After should or shouldn't, we must always use pure Verb 1 without -ing, -ed, or -s!

Challenge 1: Diagnosis & Subject Agreement! What is the correct clinical diagnosis for Jordan?
Challenge 2: The Golden Medical Law! Which sentence follows the 'Verb 1 Pure' rule (No -ing, No -ed, No -s)?
Challenge 3: Athlete Nia's Sprain! What shouldn't Nia do while recovering her sprained ankle?
Challenge 4: Have Got vs Has Got! Complete for multiple patients: "They ______ sore throats after singing."
Challenge 5: Clinical Rx Prescription! How should Dr. Ardiansyah formulate the final advice for a fever patient?
