🩺 Patient Case 1: Gymnast Nia with a Sprained Ankle (Compressing with ice & elevating leg)
🩺
DR. ARDIANSYAH, M.D. • CLINICAL FORMULARY
Patient: Nia (Gymnast) • Chief Complaint: Swollen right ankle during landing practice.

⚠️ THE GOLDEN MEDICAL LAW (THE NO-ING, NO-ED, NO-S RULE):

After modal verbs SHOULD (anjuran) and SHOULDN'T (pantangan), the action verb must always be in VERB 1 PURE!
❌ You should resting   ➔   ✔️ You should rest.
❌ He shouldn't runs   ➔   ✔️ He shouldn't run.

1. Subject Agreement: Choose the correct diagnosis for Gymnast Nia: "Nia ______ a sprained ankle."
2. Spot the Error! Which prescription follows the Golden Law of Medicine (Verb 1 Pure)?
